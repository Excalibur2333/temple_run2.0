Curse of the Lost Temple

Sprint through a shattered jungle sanctuary, vault spike pits, slide beneath fallen stone, and outrun the collapse of a forgotten ruin. This original pixel-inspired runner blends arcade reflexes with a polished temple adventure presentation.

Game Brief

You are a relic hunter fleeing the wrath of an awakened temple. Survive four escalating stages, collect relic shards for score, protect your three lives, and endure the final collapse countdown to escape with the treasure.

Controls

Space
Jump over pits, traps, cracked tiles, and rolling hazards.
Arrow Down
Duck and slide under broken pillars and low fireballs.
P
Pause or resume the expedition at any time.
R
Restart the run from Stage 1.
